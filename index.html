How it works

Is it free?

Yes. Paste a public Instagram link, and the tool fetches the media URL through a proxy and gives you a direct download.

Why do some links fail?

Private accounts, age-restricted posts, and deleted content can't be fetched. Instagram also changes its page format periodically — when it does, the extractor needs updating.

Do you store the videos?

No. The request is proxied on the fly and never written to disk.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes — it's responsive. The "Save file" button triggers your phone's download prompt.

Only download content you own or have permission to use. Respect Instagram's Terms of Service and copyright law.
